How to play

Win one of three ways

  • Conquest: own of the land, or be the last nation standing.
  • Core: keep a mine digging past level until it reaches level .
  • Space: turn a level train station into a launch site, get labs (a mine at level can become a lab) to level , then launch a rocket. Everyone sees the countdown and will try to stop you.

Controls

  • Click the map to pick where you start.
  • Left-click empty land or another nation to send troops there. The Attack force slider (bottom) sets how many of your people go.
  • Right-click your own land to build. Right-click another nation to make an alliance or attack.
  • Click one of your buildings to upgrade or delete it. Click a submarine, then the water, to move it.
  • Drag to move around, scroll to zoom, Esc to pause.

Tips

  • Your people grow fastest when your population bar is about half full. Sending everyone to fight leaves your land empty and slow to grow.
  • Taking a nation's land costs more the more crowded it is, so thinly spread nations are easier to beat.
  • Mines only go on swizz deposits. Cities raise how many people you can have.
  • When someone attacks you, a warning shows at the top right. Press Counter to fight back.
  • Allies trade with you and can send help, but attacking an ally makes you a traitor.

The game counts plays anonymously (no names or emails) so it can be made better.
